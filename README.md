# Superstore Sales Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Superstore sales data to understand business performance across regions, categories, products, and time.  
The goal is to identify sales trends, top-performing products, and seasonal patterns to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Objective

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Identify best and worst performing products
- Evaluate regional and category-wise performance
- Understand seasonal trends in sales
- Generate business insights for decision making

---

## Dataset

- Source: Kaggle Superstore Dataset  
- Link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/bhanupratapbiswas/superstore-sales  

### Features:
Order_Date, Region, Category, Sub_Category, Product_Name, Sales, etc.

Note: Profit data is not available, so analysis is based only on Sales.

---

## Key Insights

- Sales vary significantly across regions
- Certain categories contribute most revenue
- Phones and Chairs are top-performing sub-categories
- Clear seasonal trends in monthly sales
- Discounts impact sales but require optimization

---

## Best Performing Areas

- High-performing regions generate most revenue
- Few products contribute major share of sales
- Category distribution is uneven

---

## 5 Tactical Improvements

1. Focus on high-performing categories
2. Improve marketing in low-performing regions
3. Optimize discount strategies
4. Strengthen inventory for top products
5. Use seasonal trends for forecasting
